💡 How to use the Planner

  • Type a task in the input box, choose a priority level, and click Add Task.
  • Click the circle button on the left to mark a task as done.
  • Click the ✕ button on the right to remove a task permanently.
  • Use the filter buttons to view All, Pending, or Completed tasks.
  • Your tasks are saved automatically — they'll be here when you return!
